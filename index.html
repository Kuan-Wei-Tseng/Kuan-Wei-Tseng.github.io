ABOUT

I am an Application Software Engineer at Applied Intuition, where I build customer-facing features and workflows for simulation and data tooling in automotive programs.

Previously, I was a Computer Vision Engineer at Mujin, Inc., focusing on 3D object detection. I received my M.S. from Tokyo Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Ikuro Sato, and collaborated with Prof. Rei Kawakami and Prof. Satoshi Ikehata on human motion prediction with transformers. During graduate school, I also interned at Apple Japan and Denso IT Lab.

Before graduate school, I worked at NTU in the Image and Vision Lab (imLab) and AI Application and Integration Lab (AI^2 Lab) with Prof. Chu-Song Chen and Prof. Yi-Ping Hung. I also completed my B.S. in Engineering at NTU under Prof. Yi-Ping Hung. My interests are in visual computing, especially 3D computer vision, deep learning, and interactive systems.
